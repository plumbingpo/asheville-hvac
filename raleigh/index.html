Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Raleigh

Routine maintenance is the difference between a system that lasts and one that quits in the middle of a heat wave. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

The age of your equipment plays a big role. With regular service, central air usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like rising energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's time to plan a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule is cheaper and is far less stressful than an emergency swap during the first heat wave of summer.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A capacitor that's starting to fail is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet that same capacitor, ignored for a season, can ruin the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone today so help is one call away as soon as something seems off in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.
